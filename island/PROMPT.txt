Build a short cinematic 3D scene in the current empty folder: a small tropical
island in open ocean, seen in a 60-second time-lapse from late afternoon
through sunset to a starry night.

Deliverable
- Runs by serving the folder statically and opening index.html (no build step).
  Three.js from a pinned CDN version is allowed. No other external assets: all
  visuals are code-generated, all sounds via WebAudio.
- Exactly 60 seconds, plays once from a Start button, then shows Replay.
- Portrait 9:16 framing (target 1080x1920); letterbox on other aspect ratios so
  every shot keeps the same composition.
- Ocean with moving waves, foam where water meets the shore, and reflections
  that change with the light. Island with cliffs, a beach, trees that sway,
  and a lighthouse whose beam turns on at dusk.
- Sky, sun, clouds and fog change color over the 60 seconds; stars appear at
  night. A coherent palette.
- At least four camera shots with deliberate cuts or moves (for example a
  distant approach over the water, a slow orbit around the island, a low pass
  along the beach, a final pull-back as the lighthouse sweeps the night).
- Deterministic: the same time always renders the same frame (seeded
  randomness, no frame-rate dependent motion).
- Pause/resume and skip-to-next-shot with touch and keyboard.
- Smooth on a phone GPU: cap devicePixelRatio, aim for a stable 60fps.
- Expose window.__scene = { shot, time, playing, seek(t) } where seek(t) jumps
  to t seconds and renders that frame, for automated checks and frame capture.

Spend your time on visual quality and pacing. Stop when the scene is finished
and polished.
