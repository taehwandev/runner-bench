Flight Test Operations Guide

Welcome to AeroScout-1 Flight Deck. The spacecraft is fully prepped on Launch Pad 01.

Flight Phases:

  • Vertical Liftoff: VTOL thrusters spool up, ship ascends vertically to 16m hover altitude while retracting landing gear.
  • Local Test Circuit: Ship banks dynamically along the valley and canyon ridge waypoints, demonstrating coordinated turns and engine throttle.
  • Approach & Landing: Ship aligns back above the pad, deploys landing gear, descends gently, and touches down smoothly.

Interactive Controls:
