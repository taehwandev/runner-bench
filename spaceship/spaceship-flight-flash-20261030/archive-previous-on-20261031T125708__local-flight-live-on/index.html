APEX-VII Flight Test Facility

Welcome to the Pad-01 Local Flight Test console. The APEX-VII light research spaceship is pre-flight ready on the elevated landing pad.

Test Circuit Features

  • Vertical Liftoff: Dual ion thrusters spool up with heat plume & dynamic illumination, climbing vertically to 16m.
  • Local Navigation Circuit: The ship pitches and banks through 5 scenic waypoints past the North Tower, Canyon Ridge, and Coastal Station.
  • Waypoint Guidance Towers: Glowing navigational beacons in the terrain mark the designated circuit trajectory.
  • Precision Landing: Upon completing the loop, the ship automatically flares, decelerates, enters hover descent, and touches down smoothly on the central landing pad.

Camera System

  • Free Orbit: Rotate from any angle, zoom close to inspect the ship or pad, pan across the spaceport.
  • Chase Cam: Dynamic trailing camera locked to the spaceship's motion and banking turns.
  • Pad Tower: Static tower observation camera tracking the spaceship across the sky.
  • Cockpit: Pilot vantage point viewing forward over the flight horizon.

Shortcuts
