THE DEPARTURE LAB

A moment to look up.

Explore a little coastal spaceport, then send Explorer 01 toward a new horizon. Choose Aurelia Reach for a ringed planet and orbital station, or Ember Belt for a copper moon and asteroid field. The route locks during flight and can be changed before replay. Pause whenever you like, or replay the full departure after it finishes.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the camera controls to find your perspective. On a keyboard, focus the scene and use the arrow keys, + and −.

Reset restores only your camera. Pause freezes the flight; replay starts a new departure from the pad.
